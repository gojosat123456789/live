01 / About

Good design.
Clear purpose.

I’m Ashan Raza, a graphic designer with a creative approach to visual communication. I enjoy turning ideas into thoughtful, well-organized designs that are easy to understand and remember.

I create logo and branding concepts, social media graphics, posters, banners, advertisements, and packaging visuals. My design process considers the brief, intended audience, layout, typography, and colour.
