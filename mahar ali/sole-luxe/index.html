Need a hand? Get in touch ↗

How do I find my size? +

Our styles fit true to size for most customers. If you are between sizes, we recommend going up one. The size selector on each shoe shows what is currently available.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery timing depends on your location. We will confirm the estimated delivery window when we contact you about your order.

Can I change or cancel my order? +

Please contact our team as soon as possible after placing your order. Changes depend on whether your order has already been prepared for dispatch.

How can I care for my Sole Luxe sneakers? +

Wipe gently with a soft, slightly damp cloth and let your sneakers air dry away from direct heat. Check the care guidance for your particular materials.
